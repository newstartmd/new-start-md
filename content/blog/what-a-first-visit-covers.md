A first visit is a conversation with a licensed provider, not a product offer.

## What the visit is for

The provider reviews your history and your goals. They decide whether any treatment is appropriate. This page does not promise a result.

- A licensed provider makes that decision.
- Compounded medications described on this site are not FDA-approved.
- Individual results vary.

## What this note is not

This is educational only. It is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a visit.
