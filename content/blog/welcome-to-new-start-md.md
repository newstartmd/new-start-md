This journal is where New Start MD will publish plain-language notes about physician-guided peptide, hormone, and metabolic care.

## What you will find here

Posts explain how this kind of care works so you can ask better questions. They do not promise a result from any product, and they are not a substitute for a visit with a licensed provider.

- A licensed provider decides whether any treatment is appropriate.
- Compounded medications described on this site are not FDA-approved.
- Individual results vary.

## What this page is not

The Learn section stays a separate education hub. This journal is the publishing pipeline for new posts, and this first note is the first published entry.
